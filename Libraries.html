BANQ

This library deserves the hype. The airiness, variety of workspaces, and book conveyor belt are all charming.

L'Octogone

Off-the-beaten-track though it may be, the building is interesting and their $1 book sales contain some fascinating finds.

Maisonneuve

The building is more interesting than the book selection. The political sections giving strong representation to Quebec language politics and Franco-Canadian identity is interesting and makes sense in the context of the neighbourhood, though.

Saint-Michel

The architecture is largely unremarkable, but the graphic novel section is impressively global. The "regular people neighbourhood" vibe and metro accessibility are also great.

Réjean-Ducharme

The building is small, but the library is very cozy and has a nice selection of books.
