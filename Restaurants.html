Central and South American

At establishments on Saint-Denis or anywhere close-ish to downtown, expect to pay a bit more and encounter a bit more North American influence mixed in. I've been meaning to try several places in Villeray and beyond (for example, Las Brasas, Sinaloa Rolls, and Pupusería Doña Maria), but here are a few places I recommend in the meantime:
  • Ta Chido: the chapulines are good too!
  • Tacos Tin Tan
  • Bocadillo
  • Arepera
  • La Tamalera
Places I recommend a bit less: Mezcaleros (a bit fancier), Casa Azul (a bit fancier), Mañana, Arriba Burrito

Ice cream

  • Virevent: super creative flavours, chill Ahuntsic vibes.
  • Swirl: minimalist menu, but pretty trendy
  • Hoche Glacé: slightly-more-than-minimal classics; thematically related to the new wave of Montreal soft serve, but hasn't been widely publicized as such
  • Iconoglace: intensely maximalist, extremely hyped, line often extends down the block, fairly expensive, and huge portions. Still a reference point for understanding the mid-2020s MTL trend cycle, I guess.
  • Chocolat Favoris: local chain, moderately maximalist; a nice classic
